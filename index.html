About me

I am a conservation scientist with a strong modelling background. I am currently at Sapienza University of Rome in the Biodiversity and Global Change (BGC) lab, where I work in the GaP project on Key Biodiversity Areas and the expansion of Europe's protected areas.

I did my PhD at Sapienza under the supervision of Moreno Di Marco and Luca Santini, working on how to assess species' exposure to climate change in IUCN Red List assessments. I also hold an MSc in Natural Sciences and a second-level master in Statistics and Big Data.

In my free time I like hiking, reading and cooking. I am also a GRASS GIS and Linux fan.
