**sRedList**

As a part of my PhD, I have been involved in the [sRedList working group](https://sredlist.eu/#/team).  
sRedList is a user-friendly web platform designed to streamline the IUCN Red List assessment process.  

My work for the sRedList project focused on standardizing the integration of climate change responses into IUCN Red List assessments.  
Learn more [here](https://doi.org/10.1111/cobi.14227).
