    <h3>**sRedList**</h3>
    <p>
      As a part of my PhD, I have been involved in the [sRedList working group](https://sredlist.eu/#/team). sRedList is a user-friendly web platform designed to streamline the IUCN Red List assessment process. Developed in collaboration with Life Watch Italy, it provides assessors with scientific data and methods to calculate key parameters like Area of Occupancy (AOO), create distribution maps, analyze habitat trends, and assess fragmentation.
      My work for the sRedList project focused on standardizing the integration of climate change responses into IUCN Red List assessments. The methodology we devised complies with IUCN Red List guidelines and can be used to estimate species extinction risk. Learn more [here](https://doi.org/10.1111/cobi.14227).
