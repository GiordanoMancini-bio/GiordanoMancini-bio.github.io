GaP – guiding the expansion of EU protected areas

Biodiversa+ project

The GaP project is a network of universities, research institutes, NGOs and the KBA Secretariat. Its mission is to guide the expansion of the EU's protected areas under the Biodiversity Strategy for 2030, identifying priority locations through a gap analysis for threatened species and a Key Biodiversity Areas (KBA) scoping study across multiple taxonomic groups.

My work focuses on applying and testing KBA Criterion E, which identifies highly irreplaceable sites through spatial prioritisation, using habitat suitability maps for European tetrapods and insects.

sRedList – faster, standardised Red List assessments

sDiv working group · PhD project

As part of my PhD I joined the sRedList working group, which developed a user-friendly web platform to streamline the IUCN Red List assessment process with open data and models.

I developed a standard approach to include climate change responses in Red List assessments, now recognised as a top cited article in Conservation Biology.

Generation length of amphibians and reptiles

PhD project

Generation length is central to the IUCN Red List, but data were available almost only for birds and mammals. We modelled generation length for thousands of amphibians and reptiles as a function of body size, climate, life history and phylogeny, and showed the risks of underestimating it in extinction risk assessments.
